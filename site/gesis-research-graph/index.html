GESIS Research Graph

The GESIS Research Graph was one of the first working deployments of the Research Graph Augment API. GESIS – Leibniz Institute for the Social Sciences, Germany's largest infrastructure institution for the social sciences, based in Mannheim and Cologne, implemented it. The project built one connected graph of the publications, datasets and grants around the GESIS collections, and enriched that graph with links drawn from the global network of scholarly works. It put the augmentation method to work on a national research infrastructure, at a scale of 180,582 nodes.

This page is a record of the completed GESIS project. The figures below are those the project published, and they are no longer updated.
