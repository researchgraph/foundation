COVID-19 Research Graph

During the pandemic, research on COVID-19 grew by hundreds of articles a day. The COVID-19 Research Graph gathered about 250,000 research articles on coronaviruses and COVID-19, published between 1990 and July 2021, giving a global view of research on the virus, its treatment, its transmission and its social impact.

This page is a record of the completed COVID-19 project. The collection covers 1990 to July 2021, and it is no longer updated.

What the project built

The team linked each article's authors to their research organisations, using Research Graph data clusters. With the authorship and institution information in place, it built a time series of the collaboration network, which showed how research collaboration in the field grew over ten years and how research activity surged worldwide after December 2019.

The geographic spread of that activity revealed the footprint of collaboration within countries and between them, and the topics studied by institutes and countries showed how the research spread around the world. The collection was built to help assess the impact of COVID-19 through the research itself: how the virus spread, medical treatment, immunisation, national emergency responses and the effects on the global economy.
