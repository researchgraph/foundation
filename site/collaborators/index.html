About

Collaborators

Research Graph has formed as a blue sky project with the contribution of talented and smart individuals who had an interest in research collaboration networks. It is thrilling to see, this project has been successful in creating a large-scale graph of scholarly works. We even have a collaboration network inside the Research Graph, a network of experts — from Australia, Europe and Asia — who published a number of journal and conference papers about this project.

You can follow our work on GitHub/ResearchGraph.
