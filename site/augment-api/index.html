Augment API

The Research Graph Augment API was a service that turned the metadata of research objects into a collaboration network. Given a single ORCID or DOI, it returned a connected graph of the researchers, publications, datasets, grants and organisations linked to it.

This page is a record of the completed project. The service ran as a beta from 2021, the project is closed, and it has no further work planned.

The nodes followed the Research Graph schema.

01

The identifiers it supported

Identifier Entities
ORCID: https://registry.identifiers.org/registry/orcid Researcher
DOI: https://registry.identifiers.org/registry/doi Publication Research Data Organisation Grant
PubMed: https://registry.identifiers.org/registry/pubmed Publication
GRID: https://registry.identifiers.org/registry/grid Organisation
ROR: https://ror.org Organisation
ISNI: https://registry.identifiers.org/registry/isni Organisation
WikiData: https://registry.identifiers.org/registry/wikidata Organisation

02

Background

Research Graph designed the Augment API in 2017 as a cloud service to turn disconnected research information into a connected graph, and to enrich that graph with data from the global network of scholarly works. It used the Research Graph distributed network and persistent identifiers to connect publications, researchers, research datasets and grants across global infrastructures such as DataCite and open access initiatives such as Scholix.

The project began with the partners in the Research Data Alliance Working Group on Data Description Registry Interoperability. GESIS in Germany and NCI in Australia built the first implementations. Two webinars in 2018 presented that work:

  • Tracking research data footprints via integration with Research Graph, 8 March 2018. Dr Ben Evans and Dr Jingbo Wang of the National Computational Infrastructure (NCI) presented the NCI Augmented Graph and showed how researchers and third-party systems could reach and use its information.
  • Discovering Research Data Links via GESIS LOD Research Graph, 28 February 2018.

In 2021 the project opened a RESTful gateway to the service as a beta. It did not cover every function of the Research Graph augmentation services, but it made the core capability available to the Australian and international research community. Beta testers used the API free of charge for non-commercial work and received support through a private Slack channel. The beta's sample Jupyter notebooks remain on GitHub as a record.

03

Organisation disambiguation

A key strength of the Augment API was its access to the Research Graph entity resolution algorithms for organisation records. The services combined natural language processing with processes designed by domain experts to link each organisation name to the closest record in Wikidata. Wikidata was chosen for its wide coverage of organisation names in many languages and across the research, education, government, not-for-profit and commercial sectors. The underlying graph also held the connections to ROR, GRID and ISNI.
