About us

Research Graph Foundation builds the connective tissue of science. We map how research actually happens, a billion connections between people, papers, data and discoveries, and we give that map away, because knowledge that cannot be found may as well not exist. Our work helps a clinical team find the study that changes a trial, helps a climate institute see the collaborations it didn't know it had, and helps a seven-year-old understand that science is something you build, not something you're told. We are a not-for-profit, and we work in the open: free for research, always, and funded by the organisations that build on discovery. Science moves faster when it can see itself.

The Research Graph Foundation is a not-for-profit and collaborative venture to connect scholarly records across global research repositories. The Foundation contributes to developing capabilities that transform disconnected and siloed research activities into a connected network of scholarly works. This network has enabled the discovery of research collaborations, rapid synthesis of the literature, and provided a pathway to trace research impact.

We hold face-to-face meetings at the address under 'Contact Us'.

You can also follow our work at github.com/researchgraph.
